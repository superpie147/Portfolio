My own problems, defined and built by myself

I’m not an engineer. But “if the thing you need doesn’t exist, define the requirements and build it” — I apply my day job to myself.

Personal project · 2026.05 · Web app

JLPT N1 study game

Learning through play works better for me than drilling, so I built this game to help myself — and the Japanese learners around me — pick up new material. It’s set in a sushi shop: every correct answer earns a piece of sushi, words you get wrong stay in a “weak words” list for review, and every question shows how all players have scored on it. Updated since launch based on how it was actually used: the question logic was fixed and the bank grew to 201 questions.

Personal project · 2026 · Automation

Job-hunt automation with Notion × Make

While job hunting I was tracking many applications at once, and the easiest ones to lose sight of were those that had simply gone quiet. I built an application tracker in Notion and wired it to Make: any application with no movement for 30 days updates its status automatically, so my attention stays on the ones still in play.

Personal project · 2026.07 · Web app (interactive map)

Japan’s 100 Famous Castles — a visit log

I like visiting Japan’s castles, and I wanted a record of them — not a list, but a map I can open and see where I’ve been. All 100 castles sit on a hand-drawn-style map of Japan; visited ones get a vermilion seal with the date and a note. Deliberately no accounts and no backend: the data stays in your own browser, can be exported as a backup, and turns into a read-only share link for friends.

Personal project · 2026.07 · Web app × cloud database

100 Small Goals

Every year I write down 100 small goals and work through them over the months. I wanted more than a checklist: a way to keep the photo and the feeling of the moment each one was done, so that by December it reads like a memoir of the year, and can be shared by link. Google sign-in and a Supabase cloud database keep it safe across devices. The rules are “edit freely as a draft, then once published the structure locks while progress stays editable,” with access control enforced in the database layer and a full design → independent verification → fix → re-verify security pass.

Personal project · 2026.09 · PWA (installable on your phone)

What’s for dinner? — fridge-to-recipe suggestions

Opening the fridge with no idea what to cook is a nearly daily annoyance for me. Tick the ingredients you have, and the app ranks recipes by two scores — ingredients on hand and seasonings on hand — across Japanese, Taiwanese and simple Western dishes, named in Chinese and Japanese. Ingredients, seasonings and recipes are all editable in a small admin screen, and it installs on a phone’s home screen. From three static mockups I chose the “hand-drawn cooking notebook” look: grid paper, handwriting fonts, washi tape and sticky notes. The data layer is isolated so cloud sync can be swapped in later.
