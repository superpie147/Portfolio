Personal project · 2026 · Web app (interactive map)

Japan’s 100 Famous Castles — a visit log

All 100 castles pinned on a hand-drawn-style map of Japan: visited ones get a vermilion seal, plus the date and a short note. Deliberately no accounts and no backend — the data stays in your own browser, can be exported as a backup, and turns into a read-only public page with one click. It started from “the way I want to keep this record doesn’t exist,” so I wrote the spec, drew the map, and built it.

Personal project · 2026 · Web app × cloud database

100 Small Goals

Write down 100 small goals each year, tick them off over the months, add photos and notes — by December it reads like a memoir of the year. This time it wasn’t only a front end: Google sign-in, a Supabase cloud database, and rules I designed myself — edit freely as a draft, then once published the structure locks while progress stays editable — with access control enforced in the database layer, not just on screen. It was also my first full pass through a design → independent verification → fix → re-verify security review.

Personal project · 2026 · PWA (installable on your phone)

What’s for dinner? — fridge-to-recipe suggestions

Opening the fridge with no idea what to cook is a daily annoyance. Tick what you have, and the app ranks recipes by two separate scores — ingredients on hand and seasonings on hand — across Japanese, Taiwanese and simple Western dishes, named in Chinese and Japanese. Ingredients, seasonings and recipes are all editable in a small admin screen. The look is a “hand-drawn cooking notebook” — grid paper, handwriting fonts, washi tape and sticky notes — chosen from three static mockups; the data layer is isolated so cloud sync can be swapped in later.
